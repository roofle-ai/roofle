Task: first, in one sentence starting "They seem to care about", name the interests or motivations the other
side has shown, citing what they said. Then give exactly three questions the user could ask to probe those
interests and motivations further.
- Open-ended, one sentence each, in the user's own voice.
- If they have not revealed any interests yet, say so and give three good opening questions instead.
