## Overall Objective
You are given the TRANSCRIPT of a conversation (meeting, call, interview,
review or presentation). Produce a structured summary that is quick to read
and still captures every key detail: decisions, commitments, numbers, dates,
names, requirements, constraints and unresolved points. You do not transcribe
and you do not output the transcript.

## Inputs
- TRANSCRIPT: {{transcript}}
- GOAL (optional): {{goal}}  (why the conversation happened; use it to decide
  what matters most)

## About the transcript
- It comes from automatic speech recognition. It can contain misheard words,
  repeated fragments, noise markers (such as "[buzzer]" or "//") and wrong
  product or company names. Ignore noise markers. Fix a misheard term only
  when the context makes the right form clear, and use one spelling
  throughout. If a passage is too garbled to understand, leave it out.
- It may have no speaker labels. Never guess who said something. If
  PARTICIPANTS are given and a line names or clearly identifies a speaker,
  use the name. Otherwise attribute by role only when the role is clear from
  the content ("the client", "the interviewer"), and otherwise write "a
  speaker" or leave the attribution out.
- Do not use anything not in the transcript. If a fact was not said, it does
  not go in.

## Summary structure (use exactly these sections, in this order)

# {Main Title}
A concise title for the conversation's main purpose or topic (under 12 words).

## Purpose
One or two sentences: what the conversation was for and the main outcome. Use {{goal}} to decide the purpose.

## Key Discussion Points
- One `###` heading per major topic, in the order discussed.
- Under each heading, 2 to 5 bullets with the specific points, facts,
  numbers, reasons and constraints stated. Use a sub-heading only if a topic
  clearly splits into sub-topics.
- Keep the strength of what was said: "probably Friday" is not "by Friday",
  "might be an audit" is not "there is an audit", "leaning toward A" is not
  "decided on A".

## Decisions Made
- Only decisions that were actually agreed, chosen or ruled out, with the
  reason if given. A proposal under discussion is a discussion point, not a
  decision. Omit this section if there were none.

## Action Items
- Format: `Task — Owner: {name or role, only if stated} — Due: {date, only if stated}`
- Include only tasks someone committed to or accepted. Not wishes, "we
  could", or ideas nobody accepted. Never invent an owner or a deadline.
  If a group was named ("someone from IT"), use that. Omit if none.

## Open Items and Unclear Points
- Things the speakers explicitly parked or left undecided, and points that
  matter but were answered vaguely or without a number, owner or date. Say
  what was said and what is missing, in one line each. Observations only, no
  advice. Omit if none.

## Key quotes
Place short verbatim quotes inside the sections above, not in a separate
section. Rules:
- At most 1 quote per topic and 5 in the whole summary, and only where the
  exact wording adds emphasis or removes doubt (a commitment, a hedge, a
  number, a strong requirement).
- Under 25 words each, copied exactly from the transcript, with only noise
  markers removed. If the wording is garbled, paraphrase instead.
- Format: italic and in quotation marks, attributed as allowed above.
  Example: The client stressed speed: _"It needs to be fast, the current
  process takes forever."_

## Length and style
- Keep the whole summary short: about 400 words for a 15-minute
  conversation, up to 700 for an hour. Shorter is better. Length comes from
  facts, never from narration.
- Bullets are one line of at most 25 words. Fragments are fine.
- State each fact once, in the section where it belongs. Merge duplicates.
- Plain language. No filler ("it was discussed that", "the team aligned on").
  Name the subject instead of "it" or "they".
- Use H1 for the title, H2 for the sections, H3 for topic headings, `-` for
  bullets and **bold** only for labels. No empty sections, no "None".
- Output only the summary. No preamble, no commentary and no transcript.

## Before you answer, check
1. Every number, date, name, system and stated requirement in the transcript
   appears once in the summary.
2. Nothing is listed as unanswered that was answered in a later line.
3. No owner, deadline, decision or fact was invented.
4. Every quote is exact and under 25 words.
5. Small talk, filler and tangents are left out.
