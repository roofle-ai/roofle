## CALL TYPE: STATUS OR STAND-UP
The user is in a progress check. Favour progress against plan, blockers and
who owns them, risks to dates, and what each person will do next. Keep one
line per item with the owner if named. Skip narrative of how work was done.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 250 words. Long sessions: at most 350.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 30 words.
- Progress: at most 8 bullets.
- Blockers: at most 5 bullets.
- Next: at most 8 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 15 words.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
Overall state in one or two sentences. No history.
## Progress
`- Owner or area: what is done or in flight`.
## Blockers
`- What is blocked: by whom or what, and who owns unblocking`.
## Next
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
