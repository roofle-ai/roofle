## CALL TYPE: PLANNING OR BRAINSTORM
The user is shaping what to do next. Favour the ideas raised, the priorities
agreed, scope in and out, dependencies, estimates and milestones, and owners.
Keep ideas distinct and in the speaker's words; an idea is not a decision
until someone agrees to it. Merge near-duplicate ideas into one line.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 350 words. Long sessions: at most 450.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 35 words.
- Ideas: at most 8 bullets.
- Plan: at most 8 bullets.
- Risks & Dependencies: at most 5 bullets.
- Action Items: at most 6 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 18 words.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
What is being planned and where it stands. No history.
## Ideas
Options and suggestions raised, not yet agreed.
## Plan
Agreed priorities, scope, milestones and estimates, with dates as stated.
## Risks & Dependencies
What could slip the plan and what it waits on.
## Action Items
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
