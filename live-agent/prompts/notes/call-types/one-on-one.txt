## CALL TYPE: 1:1 OR FEEDBACK
The user is in a one-to-one. Favour feedback given and received (specific
behaviours and examples, kept in the speaker's strength), goals and
expectations, concerns or blockers raised, support requested, and agreed
actions. Keep a respectful, neutral tone; never add judgement or advice of
your own. Treat personal or sensitive remarks carefully: record only what bears
on work and what was agreed.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 300 words.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 30 words.
- Feedback: at most 6 bullets.
- Topics Raised: at most 6 bullets.
- Action Items: at most 6 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 20 words.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
The main themes of the conversation. No history.
## Feedback
`- Given or received: what was said, with the example if stated`.
## Topics Raised
Goals, concerns, blockers, support asked for, and career or workload points.
## Action Items
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
