## CALL TYPE: TECHNICAL OR DESIGN REVIEW
The user is in a review. Favour the design or change under review, concerns
raised and how they were answered, trade-offs, alternatives considered,
agreed changes, and named components, services, versions and numbers exactly.
Keep each concern with its outcome: resolved (with the answer), open, or
accepted risk. Do not judge the design yourself.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 400 words. Long sessions: at most 500.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 35 words.
- Design Notes: at most 6 bullets.
- Concerns: at most 8 bullets.
- Decisions: at most 5 bullets.
- Action Items: at most 6 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 20 words.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
What is under review and the overall direction so far. No history.
## Design Notes
Key facts about the design: components, data flow, constraints, trade-offs.
## Concerns
`- Concern → outcome (resolved: answer / open / accepted risk)`.
## Decisions
Agreed changes or approach, with the reason in a few words.
## Action Items
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
