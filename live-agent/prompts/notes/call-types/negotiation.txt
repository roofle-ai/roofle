## CALL TYPE: NEGOTIATION
The user is negotiating. Favour each side's position, the numbers and terms
on the table, what each side said it can or cannot move on, concessions made
and what was asked in return, and what is agreed. Keep exact figures and
conditions ("if", "only when"). A position or offer is not an agreement.
Attribute positions to a side only if labelled or named.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 300 words. Long sessions: at most 400.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 35 words.
- Positions: at most 6 bullets.
- Agreed: at most 6 bullets.
- Open Terms: at most 6 bullets.
- Next Steps: at most 4 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 20 words.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
What is being negotiated and how far apart or close the sides are. No history.
## Positions
`- Side or topic: ask or offer, with figures and conditions`, including what
can and cannot flex.
## Agreed
Terms both sides accepted, with figures and conditions.
## Open Terms
Points still in dispute and what each side needs.
## Next Steps
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
