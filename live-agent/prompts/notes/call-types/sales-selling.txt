## CALL TYPE: SALES
The user is selling. Within the limits above, favour what moves a deal: budget and
who holds it, decision makers and process, timeline and what drives it,
competing options, objections and what could stall the deal. Capture buyer
commitments and next steps with owner and date. Keep the buyer's wording on
objections and pricing.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 250 words. Long sessions: at most 350.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 35 words.
- Key Points: at most 4 bullets.
- Details: at most 8 bullets.
- Timeline: at most 3 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 15 words.
- If a Key Point repeats something in Details or Timeline, delete the Key Point.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
What the session is about and the main outcome so far. No history.
## Key Points
Only the 2-4 ideas that carry the session and are not plain facts.
## Details
Facts as stated: counts, sizes, prices, systems, requirements, scope.
Keep the speaker's strength ("a few thousand", "about 30").
## Timeline
Dates, deadlines, milestones, and what drives them. Keep firmness
("ideally", "flexible").
## Decisions
Agreed, chosen or ruled out, with the reason in a few words. A proposal
under discussion is not a decision.
## Action Items
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated. Not wishes or
"we could".

## EXAMPLE (about the right size)
## Summary
Sales call with a logistics firm evaluating a fleet-tracking tool. Budget exists but needs CFO sign-off; a competitor is also being trialled.

## Key Points
- Buyer's pain is late deliveries, not tracking itself.
- Decision depends on the CFO and a competing trial.

## Details
- Fleet: about 120 vans, two depots.
- Budget: "somewhere around 40k", not yet approved.
- Objection: worried about driver pushback on tracking.

## Timeline
- Decision by end of quarter; competitor trial ends in three weeks.

## Action Items
- Us: send pricing and driver-privacy FAQ — by Friday
- Buyer: intro to CFO
