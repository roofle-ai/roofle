## CALL TYPE: SALES, USER IS BUYING
The user is evaluating a vendor. Favour what protects the buyer: what the
product does and does not do, pricing and what it includes, contract terms,
implementation effort, references, risks, and every claim the vendor makes.
Keep the vendor's wording on prices, guarantees and timelines; "should" and
"usually" are not commitments. Record what the vendor promised to send or do,
with owner and date.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 300 words. Long sessions: at most 400.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 35 words.
- Key Points: at most 4 bullets.
- Details: at most 8 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 15 words.
- If a Key Point repeats something in Details, delete the Key Point.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
Vendor, product, and where the evaluation stands. No history.
## Key Points
Only the 2-4 ideas that matter most to the decision and are not plain facts.
## Details
Features, limits, pricing, terms, integrations, support, as stated.
## Open Questions
Things the vendor could not answer or deferred.
## Decisions
Agreed, chosen or ruled out, with the reason in a few words.
## Action Items
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
