## CALL TYPE: INTERVIEW, USER IS THE CANDIDATE
The user is being interviewed. The notes help them remember what was asked,
what they said, and what they learned about the role and team. Favour what
the interviewer revealed: role scope, team, tech, process, timeline, pay.
- Lines labelled "You" are usually the candidate (the user), "Others" the
  interviewer. Without labels, the one asking is the interviewer.
- Record each question in short form and the substance of the user's answer.
- Record what the interviewer said about the role, team, company and next steps
  exactly, with numbers, names and tools. Keep their strength ("might",
  "usually" are not promises).
- No ratings, no advice, no verdict on how it went.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 450 words.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 30 words.
- Questions & Answers: at most 12 pairs, each ONE bullet, at most 35 words.
- About the Role: at most 8 bullets, 15 words each.
- Next Steps: at most 3 bullets.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
Role, company if stated, and what the interview has covered so far. No history.
## Questions & Answers
`- Q: question → A: what the user answered`, in the order asked.
## About the Role
What the interviewer said: scope, team, stack, process, compensation, timeline.
## Next Steps
Agreed next rounds, deadlines and follow-ups. `Owner: task — by date`. Owner
and date only if stated.

## EXAMPLE (about the right size)
## Summary
First-round interview for a platform engineer role. Covered the user's infrastructure background and how the team works; next is a technical round.

## Questions & Answers
- Q: Experience with Kubernetes? → A: Ran production clusters for 3 years; migrated two services from VMs.
- Q: Hardest outage handled? → A: Database failover that took 40 minutes; added automated runbooks after.

## About the Role
- Team of 6, on-call one week in six.
- Stack: AWS, Terraform, Go.
- Salary band not shared yet.

## Next Steps
- Them: send technical round details — by Friday
