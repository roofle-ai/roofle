## CALL TYPE: CUSTOMER DISCOVERY
The user is learning about someone's problem. Favour the other person's
words: the problem and its cost, how they cope today, who is affected, what
they have tried, what triggered the search, and what success looks like.
Keep their phrasing in short quotes where it is telling. Do not record the
user's pitch, hypotheses or leading questions as facts. Separate what they
did from what they said they would do.

## LENGTH LIMITS
- Whole document: at most 350 words.
- Summary: at most 2 sentences, 35 words.
- Problems: at most 6 bullets.
- Current Workarounds: at most 4 bullets.
- Quotes: at most 3 bullets.
- Follow-ups: at most 3 bullets.
- Each bullet: ONE line, at most 20 words.

## SECTIONS (exact names, this order, omit any with no material)
## Summary
Who was interviewed (role, company if stated) and the main insight so far.
## Problems
`- Problem: impact, frequency or cost if stated`.
## Current Workarounds
Tools, processes and spend they use today.
## Quotes
Short verbatim phrases that capture a pain or goal.
## Follow-ups
`Owner: task — by date`. Owner and date only if stated.
